Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book

How far ahead can I book?

You can book with as little as 20 minutes' notice, or up to 30 days in advance.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. We don't charge cancellation fees, but please cancel as early as you can so the driver can take another job: at least 1 hour before a local trip, or at least 1 day before a long-distance trip.

How and when do I pay?

You pay at the end of your trip, and no card pre-payment is needed to book. Every driver has a card/EFTPOS facility for tap and pay. We accept card, cash and AfterPay.

Where do you operate?

Instant trips are available across the Southern Tablelands, including Goulburn, Marulan, Crookwell and Yass. Pre-orders (reservations) can be picked up from Canberra Airport, Marulan, Yass, Crookwell, Mittagong, Moss Vale, Bowral and Sydney Airport.

How do pre-orders from Canberra or the Southern Highlands work?

Book ahead and we'll allocate a driver for your trip. For Canberra, we recommend booking about 2 days in advance so a driver can be allocated. Pre-orders use the same rates and minimum fares as local trips, as shown in Pricing.

Do you do airport and long-distance trips?

Yes. Airport and long-distance trips are pre-ordered, and we recommend booking a week or more in advance.

Can I book a child seat or a lady driver?

Yes. For a child seat, add a note for the driver when you book. For a lady driver, choose the "Lady Driver" service from the list when booking.

Can I book for someone else, or for a group?

Yes, you can book for anyone. Please leave a note for the driver that the trip is for someone else. A booking can carry up to 4 passengers.

Are there extra charges for luggage, pets or waiting?

There is no charge for luggage. For pets, choose the pet service in the services list and the extra price is shown up front. For waiting, after 2 minutes the meter starts running at the per-minute rate shown in Pricing.

How do I get help or contact you?

Email us at canupikme@gmail.com. Email is our support channel at the moment.
